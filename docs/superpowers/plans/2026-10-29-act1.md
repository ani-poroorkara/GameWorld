**Goal:** Build the complete Act 1 adventure, starting execution with the village prologue.

**Architecture:** Add a pure versioned campaign model and a narrow browser adapter to the existing dependency-free canvas prototype. Integrate metadata, inventory, world layout and encounters in later verified increments. Preserve legacy journeys until an explicit Act 1 new-game path is ready.

**Tech Stack:** JavaScript UMD/CommonJS, browser canvas and localStorage, JSON, Node assert/test/VM. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-act1-design.md`, subordinate to confirmed facts in `docs/Act 1.md`.

## Global constraints

- Preserve simultaneous combat, hidden dice, per-fight use limits, one card type per round and practice save isolation.
- Punch + Hand Block at start; Pickaxe + Shield at home; Apple 5 HP; Steak 15 HP; exactly one Steak for an animal kill.
- Staff from cave victory; Throw from a separate chest; Block from a post-cave chest.
- No invented Bow/Arrow drop probabilities, upgrade recipe, child locations, kidnapper identity or Act 2 choice outcome.
- Pet Distraction is once per fight, succeeds 50%, cancels at most one attack and is spent on failure.
- Thieves spawn only on roads. Nine passages are geographic continuations. Map Cards reveal only their own settlement region.
- Keep quantity separate from level; keep unresolved content values null; draft validation is not runtime approval.
- Preserve all pre-existing uncommitted files. Do not commit or publish unrelated work. All builds remain local.
