## Intent and authority

Turn the peaceful village and surrounding wilderness into a story about choosing to help a neighbor while confronting an unresolved personal loss. Exploration should provide useful discoveries and conversations between story events, rather than longer empty walks.

The original Act 1 note remains the authority for previously agreed facts. This document supplies implementation decisions and proposed creative detail; these additions are not claims about earlier approval. The user requested expansion, a complete task plan, and beginning execution with subagents. Execution starts with the prologue milestone below. Later systems have explicit dependencies and acceptance criteria in the plan.

## Approach

Three approaches were considered: replace the entire prototype at once; build a disconnected Act 1 demo; or introduce a versioned campaign layer and integrate it incrementally. Use the campaign layer: it preserves the existing combat and practice behavior, provides isolated tests, and lets the existing village become playable early. This is an implementation choice made for this request.

Continue using dependency-free JavaScript, the existing canvas renderer, browser-local saves, JSON authoring, and Node checks. Do not replace the combat engine or add hosting services.

## Confirmed boundaries

- Start with Punch and Hand Block. Pickaxe and Shield are collected at home.
- Fourteen named card entries include the main Quest Card. Side quests may have their own quest cards.
- Apple heals 5 HP; Steak heals 15 HP. Both are consumed once per use and work in combat. Healing clamps to maximum HP.
- Preserve simultaneous commitments, per-fight use limits, hidden dice, one card type per round, and practice isolation.
- Animal victory presents spare/kill: spare clears the animal and gives no Steak; kill gives exactly one Steak, regardless of species.
- Bow uses one Arrow per committed attack; thief Arrow drops contain 5–10. Bow and Arrow probabilities remain undecided. No guaranteed first Bow.
- Pet Distraction is once per fight, succeeds with 50% probability, and cancels at most one enemy attack. A failed use is still spent.
- Cave victory awards Stone Staff. Stone Throw comes only from its own chest. Stone Block requires a chest found after cave victory.
- Thieves exist only on roads, including travel spawn destinations.
- A settlement Map Card provides knowledge only within that settlement's region. Map knowledge and physically explored tiles are separate.
- Nine mountain passages are geographic continuations. No teleportation or unrestricted world reveal.
- End Act 1 with the air monster presenting the dilemma. No actionable child-selection control, resolution, identified kidnapper, or invented child location.
- No combining recipe, automatic level benefits, or Stone Wall card.

## World and pacing

Target a 144 × 144 campaign map, subject to performance verification. Retain the existing village footprint and coordinates during the prologue preview. For the eventual new Act 1 world, translate the village template by (+32,+30), moving home from (40,42) to the map center (72,72) and initial player position to (72,74). Keep legacy coordinates for legacy journeys. Build a mountain enclosure, a safe village/farm core, forest routes and clearings, and a cracked outer region. Keep paths connected across seeds, with a road lead, cave approach, post-cave search loop and northern passage.

Place nine passage entrances around the boundary. The northern entrance is the Act 1 endpoint; other exits show locked continuation descriptions until subsequent acts exist. Crossing later unlocks the next region, rather than teleporting to an unrelated generated map. Route graph and passage coordinates belong in campaign JSON.

Use existing displayed settlement names until spellings are confirmed. Hedonville, the spoken forest name, and Cracked Lands are working labels, not silently finalized place names.

Planned density: a distinct activity or visible landmark about every 10–16 traversable tiles along intended routes. Offer short branches and return shortcuts. Do not force optional quests or random loot to unlock the main quest.

## Main story and quest model

1. **Peaceful home:** villagers describe ordinary work, farm animals wander, and the player can learn cards and obtain a local map.
2. **The request:** a neighbor explains that their child is missing. The player recalls their own lost son without supplying his fate. The player can accept or say “Not yet.” Declining preserves exploration and later acceptance.
3. **Prepare and ask:** acceptance creates the working-title Quest Card, **Save the Kid**. Its next objective asks for village information and home equipment. Retrieving gear remains optional for acceptance; previously collected gear counts.
4. **Road lead:** defeating the designated road thief supplies the cave lead independently of any item drop. No random Bow gate. Other villagers retain quest and ordinary conversation topics.
5. **Living cave:** interaction reveals that the cave is the opponent. Only victory advances this beat and grants one Staff reward.
6. **Search:** a post-cave chest provides Stone Block. A separate forest cache provides Stone Throw; neither reward is implicitly granted with the Staff.
7. **Northern passage:** after the cave, reach and confront the air monster. Present the two children as the monster's demand, without establishing where either child is or whether the demand is truthful. Freeze the quest at “Act 1 complete — decision continues in Act 2.” Exploration and journal viewing remain possible.

Store accepted state, objective progress, completed events, claimed rewards, inventory, map ownership and pending animal decisions inside saved player state. Event application must be idempotent. Out-of-order optional discoveries cannot skip prerequisites. An unaccepted quest has no Quest Card.

## Village cast: proposed staging

Use provisional names, clearly editable in content later:

- **Mara**, the neighbor: reuse the existing `mara` village resident, with new preview dialogue rather than a duplicate NPC. Practical, warm, now struggling to stay composed. Offers the request, remembers a decline, and welcomes later help.
- **Ivo**, map-giver: careful about boundaries and landmarks; supplies the local Map Card once and offers optional chest hints.
- **Tessa**, card guide: patient and concrete; explains commitment, quantities versus levels, and how equipment is collected.
- **Nell**, farmer: brisk and fond of animals; offers ordinary farm conversation and the proposed irrigation/runaway activities.
- Existing residents remain available; additional villagers are introduced as needed rather than requiring a finalized cast now.

Keep dialogue concise and use existing speaker bubbles with action buttons. Escape authored text before rendering. Do not reveal the cause of the kidnapping or imply a known fate for the player's son.

## Cards, inventory and resolution

Use canonical IDs from `content/act1.json`, quantities shown as numbers, and separate level fields. Equipment quantities do not create duplicate plays in a round. Initial equipment is level 1; levels have no invented progression mechanic.

Separate a campaign inventory from legacy `ownedCards`, which currently only understands fire/herb. Old saves retain their legacy journey and may explicitly start a new Act 1 journey later. During the prologue milestone, campaign progress is an opt-in, separately labelled preview within the existing world; it must not claim the legacy battles already obey the new inventory.

Final healing proposal: commit food with the rest of the hand, validate ownership before resolution, consume exactly once after a valid commitment, and let the existing simultaneous resolver determine healing/damage together. An invalid hand spends nothing. Full-health food handling must be documented and tested in the combat task. Food and ammo spent during practice are restored with its checkpoint.

Do not turn null combat powers or probabilities into zero or assumed defaults. Add explicit runtime publication status to content before full catalogue integration; validated draft data is not automatically playable content.

## Chests and exploration rewards

Proposed placements: an optional village-edge teaching cache with Apple; a forest fork cache with Stone Throw; a cave rear shelf chest with Stone Block, unlocked only after victory; supply caches with existing cards or tips. Each chest has a stable ID, prerequisites, one-time claim and optional NPC hint. No unapproved Bow or Arrow probability is needed for these fixed rewards.

## Milestones and completion

**Milestone 1, execute now:** pure tested campaign state; opt-in village prologue with acceptance/reacceptance, journal/card views, home gear and local map; independent review, regression checks and regenerated local builds. This begins the full plan and is not the complete act.

**Milestones 2–5:** approved inventory/combat integration, connected expanded world, main story and persistent rewards, side quests/puzzles and content editor support. The plan contains every task and its dependencies.

Act 1 is complete only when a fresh player can follow acceptance → road lead → living cave → optional search → northern air opponent → unresolved dilemma, with reload-safe progress and all confirmed mechanics. Current milestone completion must never be reported as full Act 1 completion.

## Risks and verification

The runtime overrides combat and UI functions through ordered scripts; tests must exercise the final loaded functions. Practice restores whole state and must restore campaign state too. Initial world generation happens while game.js loads, so adapters must initialize safely for both already-generated and new worlds. The working tree already contains substantial user changes: preserve them, keep new edits scoped, and do not stage, commit or publish them wholesale.

Use Node state-machine tests, existing VM gameplay tests, editor model/server tests, public bundle checks, and browser checks for new dialogue/journal controls. Performance-test the larger map before committing its dimensions. No deployment is included in this request.
